About Our Academy

Welcome to Hema Dance Academy, a place where passion, creativity and talent come together.

Our academy provides professional dance training for beginners, intermediate and advanced dancers.

We teach different dance styles including Classical, Hip Hop, Bollywood, Contemporary and Group Dance.

Our goal is to help every student develop confidence, discipline, creativity and excellent dancing skills.
